Special Foods of Kerala

Sadya

Traditional vegetarian feast served on a banana leaf.

Malabar Biriyani

Fragrant rice cooked with spices and meat.

Appam & Stew

Soft rice pancakes with coconut-based stew.

Puttu & Kadala

Steamed rice flour with spicy black chickpea curry.

Karimeen Pollichathu

Pearl spot fish wrapped in banana leaf.

Payasam

Sweet dessert made with milk or jaggery.

Malabar Parotta

Layered flaky flatbread served with curry.

Kerala Culture

Kathakali

Classical dance-drama with elaborate makeup.

Mohiniyattam

Graceful classical dance performed by women.

Kalaripayattu

Ancient martial art of Kerala.

Vallam Kali

Traditional snake boat races.

Festivals of Kerala

Onam

Harvest festival celebrated with Sadya and boat races.

Vishu

Kerala New Year symbolizing prosperity.

Thrissur Pooram

Grand festival with elephants and fireworks.

Attukal Pongala

Largest women gathering for a religious ritual.

Theyyam

Ritual art form with vibrant costumes.
