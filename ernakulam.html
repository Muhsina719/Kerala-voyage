Top Attractions in Ernakulam

Marine Drive

Beautiful waterfront promenade famous for sunsets and city views.

Fort Kochi

Historic area with colonial buildings and Chinese fishing nets.

Cherai Beach

Clean sandy beach ideal for swimming and dolphin spotting.

Hill Palace Museum

Largest archaeological museum in Kerala, once a royal palace.

Mattancherry Palace

Also known as Dutch Palace, famous for Kerala murals.

Jew Town & Synagogue

Historic street with antique shops and ancient synagogue.

Bolgatty Island

Scenic island with palace, gardens, and backwater views.

Kerala Folklore Museum

Showcases traditional Kerala art, culture, and architecture.
