Top Attractions in Kozhikode

Kappad Beach

Historic beach where Vasco da Gama first landed in India.

Beypore Beach

Famous for its ancient shipbuilding yards and stone pier.

Pazhassi Raja Museum

Archaeological museum showcasing Kerala’s royal heritage.

Sarovaram Bio Park

Eco-friendly park ideal for relaxing walks and boating.

Thusharagiri Waterfalls

Scenic waterfalls surrounded by forests and trekking trails.

Mananchira Square

Historic city park with musical fountains and greenery.
