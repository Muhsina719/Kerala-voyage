Top Attractions in Wayanad

Chembra Peak

The highest peak in Wayanad, famous for trekking and the heart-shaped lake.

Edakkal Caves

Ancient caves with prehistoric carvings and inscriptions.

Banasura Sagar Dam

India’s largest earthen dam surrounded by scenic hills and greenery.

Soochipara Waterfalls

Beautiful three-tiered waterfall ideal for sightseeing and photography.

Kuruva Island

Protected river delta with lush forests and bamboo rafting.

Wildlife Sanctuary

Home to elephants, deer, birds, and rich biodiversity.
