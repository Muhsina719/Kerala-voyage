Top Attractions in Idukki

Munnar

Famous hill station known for tea plantations, misty valleys, and cool climate.

Meesapulimala

Second-highest peak in Kerala and a paradise for trekking lovers.

Idukki Dam

One of Asia’s largest arch dams surrounded by scenic mountains.

Anchuruli Tunnel

A stunning tunnel surrounded by lush greenery and waterfalls.

Kalvari Mount

A peaceful viewpoint offering breathtaking views of the Idukki reservoir.

Wildlife Sanctuary

Home to elephants, deer, and rare bird species amid dense forests.
