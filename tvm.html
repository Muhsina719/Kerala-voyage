Top Attractions in Thiruvananthapuram

Sree Padmanabhaswamy Temple

One of the richest and most famous temples in the world, known for its Dravidian architecture.

Kovalam Beach

Popular beach destination with crescent-shaped shores and stunning sunsets.

Varkala Cliff

Unique cliff formation overlooking the Arabian Sea, ideal for relaxation and views.

Poovar Island

Serene island with golden beaches, backwaters, and lush coconut groves.

Napier Museum & Zoo

Historic museum and zoological park showcasing art, culture, and wildlife.
